# BUSINESS AI GOD

BUSINESS AI GOD (BAIG) is a Universal Business, Market & Economic Intelligence System.

## Purpose

BAIG is designed to research, understand, analyze, test, and monitor:

- Financial markets
- Physical commerce
- Agriculture
- Commodities
- Trade
- Supply chains
- Businesses
- Economic activity

The system will combine knowledge, live data, research, testing, risk management, and controlled agent intelligence.
